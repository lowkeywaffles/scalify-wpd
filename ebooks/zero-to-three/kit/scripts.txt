ZERO TO THREE - SCRIPTS (copy, then replace the [brackets])

=== Write your offer sentence ===
I make [number] short promo videos for [type of business]
that [result they want], delivered in [days] days,
for [price].

=== Find your own floor price ===
Hours for one ad (be honest, include revisions)   = ___
Minimum you accept per hour                       = ___
Floor price = hours × hourly minimum              = ___
Add 20% for messages, exports, fixes              = ___  ← never go below this

=== The message ===
Hey [name], quick one. I've started making short promo
videos for [type of business]. Here are 3 I made: [link]

Do you know anyone who runs a [type of business] who
might want something like this? I'm taking 3 launch clients
at a lower price this month.

No worries if nobody comes to mind!

=== If they say "I might need one myself" ===
That would be great. What's the main thing you want more of
right now, bookings, orders, or walk-ins? I'll suggest the
video that fits and send you the price.

=== The four-line message ===
Hi [name], your [specific post] caught my eye, [the
true observation].

I make short promo videos for [lane]. I think a 15-second
version showing [idea] would do well for you.

Examples of my work: [link]

Want me to put together a quick concept for [business
name]? No cost for the concept.

=== Then send a one-screen proposal ===
Thanks [name]. Here's what I'd suggest for [business]:

GOAL: more [bookings] for [offer]
WHAT: 3 vertical ads (15–20 s), captions and music included,
      each with a different opening
YOU SEND: 8 short phone clips (I'll send a shot list) + logo
TIMING: first drafts 5 days after I get the clips
REVISIONS: one round included
PRICE: $150 (launch price, 3 spots). 50% to start, 50% on
       delivery.

Shall I send the shot list and payment details?

=== The shot list you send them ===
Please film these on your phone, upright (vertical),
5–8 seconds each, in good light. Don't worry about sound.

1. The front of the business from outside
2. Wide shot of the inside
3. Your product/service close up, in action (3 clips)
4. A happy customer or the finished result
5. You or your team, smiling at camera
6. Anything that makes you different

Also send: your logo, your offer in one sentence,
and 1–2 customer reviews I can quote.

=== 1. Ask for a testimonial the moment they're pleased ===
So glad you like them! Could you write 2–3 sentences I can
share: what you needed, what it was like working with me,
and what you think of the result? A short voice note works
too and I'll type it up for your OK.

=== 2. Ask for one introduction ===
One more thing: do you know one other business owner who
could use videos like these? If you introduce us and they
book, your next video is 20% off.

=== 3. Offer the monthly plan when results show ===
The offer ad got [their number] views, nice. Businesses that
post new video every week tend to keep that going. I can do
8 videos a month for $300, two delivered each week, so you
never have to think about it. Want to try one month?
