# First-customer onboarding guide

Send this after purchase (or walk through it on a 15-minute call).

1. **Create your account** at [YOUR-DOMAIN]/signup and confirm your email.
2. **Set up (1 minute):** business name, industry, currency, typical quote value, preferred channel.
3. **Unlock Founding Access:** *Access* → paste your Whop membership ID (`mem_…`, found in your Whop purchases). If it fails, email us — we can activate manually after checking the payment.
4. **Add your quotes:** start with the last 30 days. Add older ones too — they're the ones most likely forgotten. Have a spreadsheet? *Import* → download the sample CSV → match columns → fix any listed row errors.
5. **Each morning:** open *Today*. Pick the top quote → choose a tone → edit → *Copy* or *Open WhatsApp/email* → send it yourself → *I sent this follow-up*.
6. **When they reply:** *Record customer response* → set the status. If they accept and you think the follow-up helped, tick the box (it's your judgement — we label it that way).
7. **If a customer asks you to stop:** mark *Customer asked not to be contacted*. QuoteRevive stops suggesting follow-ups.
8. **Settings:** adjust days-before-first-follow-up, interval and the stop-after limit.

Pilot check-in after 7 days: What did you follow up on? Anything confusing? What's missing?
