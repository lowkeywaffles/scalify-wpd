Questions

Good things to know first.

Does Brimful hold my money?

No. Brimful is a tracker. Your money stays wherever you keep it: a bank, Telebirr, an envelope, a real jar. Brimful doesn't hold, move or invest anything, and nothing in it is financial advice.

What happens if I lose my phone or clear my browser?

Your jars are stored on your device, so without a backup they would be gone. Turn on Settings → Cloud backup and keep your recovery code safe, or use Download a backup now and then. Either one restores on any device.

Is it an app I install?

It runs in your browser, and you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app and works offline. On iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen; on Android use the browser menu → Install app.

Which currencies work?

Birr, dollars, euros, pounds, shillings, naira, rupees and more. Pick yours in Settings. Brimful doesn't convert between currencies.

How do refunds work?

Email us within 14 days of paying with your licence key and we send the full amount back: to the same Telebirr number or bank account, or to your card through Whop. Details in the refund policy.

Can I save with my partner or friends?

Yes, with Save Together. Start a shared jar for an apartment, a car, a wedding or a trip, and send the invite link to up to 5 people. Everyone adds what they can and the jar fills live on every phone. Starting a shared jar needs Plus; people you invite join free (free members can add up to 15 deposits). Names and amounts are encrypted with the invite link, and no money moves through Brimful.

How old do I need to be?

Brimful is for ages 13 and up. To buy Plus you confirm that you're 18 or older, or that a parent or guardian agreed. Parents can ask us for a refund.
