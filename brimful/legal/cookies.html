Storage & Cookies

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Plain-language draft. We wrote this to be honest and readable, but it has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. If anything is unclear, email contact@scalifywpd.tech.

No cookies

Brimful sets no cookies and loads nothing from advertising or analytics companies, so there is no cookie banner to accept or reject. If you pay by card, Whop's checkout page (on whop.com) has its own cookie policy.

What is stored in your browser

To work at all, and to work offline, Brimful keeps the following on your device. All of it is strictly necessary for features you asked for, and none of it is used to track you.

NameTypePurposeLifetimeWho can read it
brimful:v1Local storageYour jars, history, challenges, settings, licence key, your recovery code if cloud backup is on, and the invite keys and member secrets for shared jars you're inUntil you delete itOnly this site, on this device
brimful-v… (the number changes with each app version)Cache storage (service worker)Copies of the app's own files so it opens without internetUntil a new version replaces it, or you clear site dataOnly this site, on this device

How to clear it

In the app: Settings → "Delete everything on this device". Or in your browser: Settings → Site data → brimful.tech → Clear.

If this changes

If we ever add anything that isn't strictly necessary (for example usage statistics), we will ask for your consent first, with "No" as easy as "Yes", and update this page.
