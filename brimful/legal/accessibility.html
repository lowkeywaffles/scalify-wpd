Accessibility

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Plain-language draft. We wrote this to be honest and readable, but it has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. If anything is unclear, email skelifywpd@gmail.com.

Our aim

We want Brimful to be usable by everyone, and we build towards the WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines. We don't claim to meet every point yet.

What we've done

  • Everything works with a keyboard, with visible focus outlines and a "Skip to content" link.
  • The 3D jar is decoration: every amount and percentage is also shown as text, and screen readers skip the picture.
  • Challenge tiles are real buttons that announce their number, amount and whether they're done.
  • Light and dark themes are checked for text contrast.
  • Motion: the app follows your device's "reduce motion" setting, and Settings → 3D motion → Reduced turns off falling coins, tilting and flipping. Sounds can be switched off.
  • Text can be zoomed, and forms explain errors in words next to the field.

Known limits

  • We test with automated tools and by keyboard; we have not yet completed testing with every screen reader.
  • The app is in English only for now.

Tell us

If something gets in your way, email skelifywpd@gmail.com and tell us what you were trying to do and on which device. We aim to reply within 5 business days.
