Refund Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Plain-language draft. We wrote this to be honest and readable, but it has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. If anything is unclear, email contact@scalifywpd.tech.

14 days, full refund

If Brimful Plus isn't for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of paying. You don't need to give a reason.

How to ask

Email contact@scalifywpd.tech with your licence key (Settings → Licence details), your transaction ID, or your Whop receipt. A parent or guardian can ask on a child's behalf.

How you get the money back

Birr payments: we send the full amount you paid back to the same Telebirr number or bank account within 5 business days of your request. If Telebirr or the bank charges a fee for sending the refund, we pay it.

Card payments: we refund the full amount through Whop to the card you used, within 5 business days of your request. Your bank then usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show it.

What happens to the app

After a refund, Plus is switched off for that licence key and the app returns to the Free plan. None of your jars, history or challenges are deleted or locked; you just can't create new ones beyond the Free limits.

After 14 days

We decide later requests case by case, and we will always refund if Brimful is faulty or not as described. Nothing in this policy limits rights the law gives you.

If a payment wasn't recognised

If you paid but Plus wasn't unlocked, email us with the transaction ID. We'll either unlock it or return your money.
