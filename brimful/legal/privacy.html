Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Plain-language draft. We wrote this to be honest and readable, but it has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. If anything is unclear, email skelifywpd@gmail.com.

The short version

Brimful is built so that we know almost nothing about you. Your jars, amounts, notes and history are stored on your own device. They are never sent to us in a form we can read: if you turn on the optional cloud backup, your device locks (encrypts) the copy with your recovery code before it is uploaded, and we can't open it. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who we are

Brimful is made and run by SCALIFY WPD, a small team based in Bole, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia (operated by an individual; not yet a registered company). Contact: skelifywpd@gmail.com. We are the "controller" of the small amount of personal data described below.

What stays on your device

Jar names, targets, dates, deposits and withdrawals, notes, challenges, your split percentages and your settings (currency, theme, motion, sound) are saved in your browser's local storage. If you turn on cloud backup, your recovery code is saved there too, so the app can keep the backup up to date. If you use Save Together, the invite keys and your member secret for each shared jar are saved there as well. We cannot see, copy or recover any of this. If you clear your browser data or lose the device, it is gone unless you downloaded a backup file or turned on cloud backup and kept your recovery code.

Who helps us run it

  • Vercel hosts the website and Scalify's payments service (scalify-wpd.vercel.app). The payments service currently runs in the United States; the database it uses is in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Supabase stores the payment records and encrypted backups listed above (database region: EU, Frankfurt).
  • Whop processes card payments under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Telebirr (Ethio telecom) or Bank of Abyssinia process your transfer under their own terms. We see what appears on our own statement.
  • Telegram delivers a message to us when a payment arrives. It says only the product, amount and payment method: never your name, transaction ID or licence key.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone else. These providers may process data outside your country, including the EU and the United States.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your app data: Settings → "Delete everything on this device", or clear your browser data.
  • Take your data with you: Settings → "Download a backup".
  • Payment records: email us to ask for a copy, a correction or deletion. We'll answer within 30 days. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU or UK), you may also have the right to object, to restrict use, and to complain to your data-protection authority.

Children

Brimful is for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at younger children. The app itself collects no personal data from anyone. To buy Plus you must confirm that you are 18 or older, or that a parent or guardian has agreed. A parent or guardian can ask us for a refund under the Refund Policy. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us payment details, email us and we will delete them.

Security

Payment records are stored in a database only our server can read. Cloud backups are encrypted on your device with AES-256 using a key made from your 20-character recovery code; the code never leaves your device, so neither we nor our providers can read a backup, and if you lose the code nobody can recover it. Shared jars are encrypted the same way with a random key that is part of the invite link (after the "#", which browsers don't send to servers). Anyone who has the link can open and join the jar, so share it only with people you trust; the person who made the jar can remove members. If a backup file is shared, the invite keys inside it are shared too, so keep backup files private. Your app data's safety also depends on your device: anyone who can unlock your phone and open your browser can open Brimful.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
